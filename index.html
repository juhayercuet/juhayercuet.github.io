Featured work

ACI EcoConcrete Student Competition 2025

8th place · international

Design and life cycle analysis of eco-concrete using SCMs under transportation and virgin-material constraints

After winning the internal CUET round, our six-member team (CUETX) represented CUET at the American Concrete Institute's international student competition. We designed a concrete mix to ACI 211.1 using only materials sourced within the competition's transport limits, then optimized 39 trial blends of fly ash, silica fume and GGBS across three sourcing scenarios.

We modelled chloride-induced service life in Life-365 and ran a cradle-to-gate LCA across five impact categories. The final mix replaced 40% of cement with fly ash from the Matarbari power plant.

Faculty advisor: Prof. Dr. G. M. Sadiqul Islam · ACI announcement

Hospital waste flow analysis

B.Sc. thesis · 2024–2025

Negotiated access to confidential records and weighed seven days of waste at nine hospitals, then obtained incineration logs from Chattogram City Corporation. A STAN material flow analysis estimated that of 42.9 t generated in one week, only 5.6 t reached the city's medical waste incinerator.

Read the thesis (DOI)

Rice husk ash soil stabilization

Geotechnical lab, CUET · ICCESD 2026

Ran standard Proctor and unconfined compressive strength tests on 108 sand specimens with 3–9% cement and 0–15% rice husk ash. The 9% cement + 10% RHA mix gave the highest 28-day strength, and RHA shifted failure from brittle to more ductile.

Read the paper

Pervious concrete for groundwater recharge

Champion · ASCE CUET World Water Day 2022

A literature-based proposal for pervious geopolymer pavements using biomedical waste ash and recycled aggregate, aimed at Dhaka's falling groundwater table and urban waterlogging.

Leadership and service

Rebuilding a flood-damaged home

ACI Student Chapter CUET · Fatikchhari, Chattogram · Aug 2024 – Jan 2025

After the August 2024 floods, I led the chapter as President in rebuilding a permanent home for a family in Fatikchhari. We surveyed the damaged house, prepared low-cost architectural and engineering layouts with local materials, raised funds with the Upazila administration, faculty and alumni, recruited labour and ran weekly quality visits. The house was handed over on 2 January 2025, and the project was nominated for the ACI Annual Magazine 2024.
